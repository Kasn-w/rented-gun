# Rented Gun
*Built May 2026*

A vampire survivors clone but you have to pay for the guns you rent every 15 seconds. Try to survive 5 minutes, you'll lose if you don't have enough money to pay rent

![gameplay screenshot](_forREADME/preview.gif)

## About
Built solo with [Godot 4.6.2](https://godotengine.org/) in 72 hours for [Solo Dev Jam #11](https://itch.io/jam/solo-dev-jam-11), under the theme "Everything Has a Cost".

## How to Play
- Control
    - `WASD` to move
    - `E` to open shop menu
    - `Mouse` to buy, click
- Goal
    - Survive for 5 minutes
